
## Business Processes
Nordic Supply AB operates through six core business processes.
### 3.1 Order-to-Cash (O2C)
Responsible Department: Sales & Finance
1. Customer submits an order.
2. Sales representative creates a sales order.
3. Inventory availability is checked.
4. Warehouse picks and prepares products.
5. Goods are shipped to the customer.
6. Sales invoice is issued.
7. Customer payment is registered.
8. Finance reconciles the transaction.

### 3.2 Procure-to-Pay (P2P)

Responsible Department: Procurement & Finance
1. Inventory levels or customer demand trigger purchasing.
2. Buyer identifies an approved supplier.
3. Purchase order is created.
4. Supplier confirms the order.
5. Goods are received at the warehouse.
6. Inventory records are updated.
7. Supplier invoice is registered.
8. Finance processes supplier payment.

### 3.3 Inventory Management

Responsible Department: Warehouse & Logistics
- Goods receiving and put-away.
- Inventory tracking and stock adjustments.
- Warehouse transfers.
- Reorder point management.
- Stocktaking and inventory reconciliation.
- Picking, packing and dispatch.

### 3.4 Financial Management

Responsible Department: Finance & Administration

- General ledger management.
- Accounts payable.
- Accounts receivable.
- VAT reporting.
- Bank reconciliation.
- Financial closing and reporting.

### 3.5 Supplier Management

Responsible Department: Procurement

- Supplier onboarding.
- Supplier evaluation.
- Purchase agreements and pricing.
- Purchase history tracking.
- Supplier performance monitoring.

### 3.6 Customer Management

Responsible Department: Sales & Customer Relations

- Customer registration.
- Credit limit management.
- Customer-specific pricing.
- Sales history tracking.
- Customer segmentation.
- Relationship management.
