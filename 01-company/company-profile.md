### Business Description
Nordic Supply AB is a Swedish B2B wholesale distribution company specialising in workplace equipment, office supplies and business technology.

The company sources products from European manufacturers and distributes them to businesses across Sweden.

Its customer base consists primarily of SMEs, corporate offices, educational institutions and public-sector organisations.

### Business Objectives
- Expand geographical operations across Sweden.
- Establish a scalable and integrated business infrastructure.
- Improve procurement and inventory management.
- Standardise operational and financial processes.
- Improve data accessibility and business reporting.
- Support data-driven decision-making.
